The Process

Adoption in Three Simple Steps

Our thoughtful process connects loving families with their perfect pets.

1

Browse & Discover

Explore 24+ pets across 6 categories. Use smart filters to find animals that match your lifestyle, space, and experience level perfectly.

2

Apply to Adopt

Complete our thoughtful application. We ask about your home and lifestyle — not to exclude you, but to find the best possible match for you and your pet.

3

Welcome Home!

After approval, bring your new family member home. Our team provides ongoing guidance to help you and your pet settle in happily together.
